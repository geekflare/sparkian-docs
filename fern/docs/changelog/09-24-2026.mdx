## Workspace analytics

**Settings → Analytics** now shows how your workspace uses Sparkian: messages and Sparks over time, your most active chats and members, which models are used most, and what your workspace contains. Filter by date range, model, member, or project. See [Analytics](/analytics).

## Sparks consumed per message

Every response now shows how many Sparks it used. Hover the ✦ icon in a response's action row to see the exact amount. See [Sparks (credit system)](/sparks).
