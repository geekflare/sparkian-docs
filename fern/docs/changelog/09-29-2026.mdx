## Listen to a response

Every response now has a **Listen to response** action, so you can have it read aloud instead of reading it yourself. See [Chat response actions](/chat-response-actions).

## Jump between prompts in a conversation

Conversations with more than one prompt now show a small navigator along the edge of the chat — hover a mark to preview that prompt, or click to jump straight to it. See [Jumping between prompts](/conversation-prompts).
