Open **Settings → Analytics** to see usage for your current [workspace](/workspaces). By default it shows the last 30 days.

<Frame caption="Settings → Analytics">
  <img src="https://cdn.sparkian.com/docs/analytics.png" alt="Settings Analytics tab"/>
</Frame>

## What you'll see

- **Overview** — members, Sparks available and used, messages sent and received, and how many models were used
- **Usage** — messages over time and Sparks spent over time
- **Member activity** and **Top 10 chats** — who's active, and which chats used the most Sparks (with message count, date, and models)
- **Team** — top members with messages sent, AI responses, Sparks used, and last active date
- **Model breakdown** — which models are used most, and which ones consume the most Sparks
- **Workspace content** — how many conversations, messages, projects, images, knowledge sources, artifacts, and attachments your workspace has

## Filtering

Click **Filters** to narrow usage by **date range** (up to one year, in UTC), **model**, **member**, or **project**. Workspace totals stay unchanged.
