Open your account menu (bottom of the sidebar) → **Settings**. It has three tabs: **General**, **Analytics**, and **Billing**.

## General

- **Account** — your avatar, full name, and sign-in email (contact support to change your email), and **Log out** of the current session
- **Appearance** — choose how Sparkian looks: **System**, **Light**, or **Dark**

## Analytics

Usage for your current workspace: messages, Sparks spent, top chats, team activity, and model breakdown. See [Analytics](/analytics).

## Billing

Your current [plan](/plans), Sparks balance and renewal date, usage against your plan's seats/workspaces/knowledge base limits, and options to upgrade or add Sparks.

## Deleting your account

Under **General → Danger zone**, **Delete account** permanently deletes your account and any workspaces only you use.

<Frame caption="Account settings">
  <img src="https://cdn.sparkian.com/docs/settings.png" alt="account settings"/>
</Frame>

<Warning>
This can't be undone. If you're the only person using a workspace, it's deleted along with your account. If a workspace has other members, transfer ownership to someone else first — otherwise, if no one takes over billing, any active subscription on it is canceled. Type **DELETE** to confirm.
</Warning>
