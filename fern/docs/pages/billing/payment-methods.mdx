Sparkian uses [Stripe](https://stripe.com) to process all payments. Stripe is a PCI-compliant payment processor used by millions of businesses worldwide, so your payment details are handled securely end to end.

<Note>
Sparkian does not store your full credit card details on its servers. All payments are processed securely by Stripe.
</Note>

## Accepted payment methods

- **Credit and debit cards** - Visa, Mastercard, American Express
- **Digital wallets** - Google Pay, Apple Pay, PayPal

## Supported currencies

Prices are set in USD, but Stripe automatically converts and charges in your local currency where supported, including:

- GBP
- EUR
- SGD
- INR
- AED
- CAD

Additional currencies may be available depending on Stripe's regional coverage.

## UPI for India

Users in India can also pay via **UPI** (Unified Payments Interface), letting you complete checkout directly from any UPI-enabled app.
