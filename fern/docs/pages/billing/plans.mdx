Sparkian offers a **Free** plan and paid tiers. Every plan runs on the same [Sparks](/sparks) system. The paid plans include a larger monthly Spark allowance, plus higher limits on things like knowledge base storage, chat history, seats, and file uploads.

<Note>
For current plan names, prices, and exact limits, see [sparkian.com/pricing](https://sparkian.com/pricing).
</Note>

## How plan Sparks work

- Your plan's monthly Spark allowance refreshes each billing period and does **not** roll over.
- You can buy a one-time **Spark top-up** on any plan, including Free, if you need more before your next refresh. Purchased Sparks expire one year after purchase and are used only after your plan's monthly allowance runs out.

## Model access

Some models are limited by plan. In the model picker, a model shows:

- Available to select
- **Locked** requires a paid plan


## Seats and workspaces

Paid plans include a set number of seats and workspaces, with the option to add extra seats for a per-seat monthly fee. See [Workspaces](/workspaces) for how members and roles work once you have a seat.
