---
title: Jumping between prompts
subtitle: Navigate a long conversation without scrolling through it.
slug: conversation-prompts
---

Once a conversation has multiple prompts, Sparkian shows a small navigator along the right edge of the chat like table of contents.

<Frame caption="Conversation prompts navigator">
  <img src="https://cdn.sparkian.com/docs/conversation-prompts.png" alt="Conversation prompts navigator"/>
</Frame>

Hover a mark to preview that prompt, or click it to jump straight to that point in the conversation. It is useful for long threads where scrolling back to find an earlier question would take a while.

<Note>
This navigator only appears on wider screens.
</Note>
