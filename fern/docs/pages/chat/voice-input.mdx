---
title: Voice input
subtitle: Dictate your message instead of typing it.
slug: voice-input
---

Click the microphone icon in the message box, or open the **+** (Add tools and actions) menu and choose **Voice input** (or type `/voice`), to dictate your message instead of typing it. Your browser will ask for microphone permission the first time you use it.
