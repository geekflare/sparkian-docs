You can [try Sparkian](https://sparkian.com/chat) without signing up. As a guest, you get 5 messages a day on Lite models, and your chats stay in that browser only.

## Importing your guest chats

When you sign up or sign in, Sparkian offers to import any chats from your guest session into your account.

<Frame caption="Import guest chats">
  <img src="https://cdn.sparkian.com/docs/import-guest-chats.png" alt="Import guest chats"/>
</Frame>

Pick which chats to keep, then **Import**. Imported chats are removed from the browser's guest session.
