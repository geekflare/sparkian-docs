**Sparkian** is the new name for Geekflare Chat. Sparkian is an AI workspace to chat, generate images, audio, and video, and delegate work to agents. Right now, chat and image generation are live; audio, video, and agents are on the way.

Sparkian is a **multi-chat platform** to chat with 50+ models from multiple AI providers, run multiple LLMs side by side to compare answers, and let Sparkian automatically pull in web search or website info when your question needs it.
